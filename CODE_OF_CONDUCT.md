# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the Cloudera Data Platform (CDP) Terraform Provider community to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive place for everyone. Contributors, maintainers, users, and other participants are expected to help create an environment free from harassment, discrimination, and intimidation, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone participating in the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful, including when disagreeing.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for the impact of your words and actions.
- Offer constructive, actionable feedback about code, documentation, design, and decisions rather than personal criticism.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people with different experience levels.
- Respect other participants' time, boundaries, and privacy.
- Give appropriate credit for ideas, code, and other contributions.
- Accept moderation decisions and cooperate in resolving conflicts.
- Prioritize the security and safety of users when discussing vulnerabilities or operational incidents.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes, but is not limited to:

- Harassment, bullying, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory or derogatory comments, slurs, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately inflammatory communication.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Impersonation, malicious misrepresentation, or deliberately misleading other contributors.
- Repeated unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or assists in its review.
- Knowingly disclosing credentials, customer data, or other sensitive information in public project spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and project-related communications. It also applies to public interactions in which someone is explicitly representing this project or its community.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness conduct that may violate this policy, contact the project maintainers **privately** through [GitHub's abuse reporting mechanisms](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Do not publish sensitive reports or personal information in a public issue.

Reports should include, when safe and available, a description of the incident, relevant dates, links or screenshots, and whether immediate protective action is needed. You may omit details that would put you at risk.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and impartially as reasonably possible, protect confidentiality to the extent practical, and limit sharing to people who need the information to respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, particularly where disclosure is legally required or necessary for safety.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, responses may include a private warning, requests to change behavior, removal or editing of content, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban from project spaces. Serious threats may be escalated to appropriate platform or organizational authorities.

Maintainers should avoid conflicts of interest when handling reports. A report involving a maintainer should be reviewed by another appropriate maintainer or organizational contact where possible. Retaliation is prohibited.

## Good-faith disagreement

Technical disagreement, respectful criticism, and decisions not to accept a proposed contribution do not by themselves violate this Code of Conduct. Discussions should stay focused on evidence, trade-offs, and the project's goals.

## Updates

Maintainers may revise this policy as the community evolves. Changes should be proposed and reviewed transparently, except where confidentiality or safety requires otherwise.
